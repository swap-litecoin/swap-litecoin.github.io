How to swap Litecoin

To swap Litecoin, choose the coin you want, give a receiving address, send your LTC in one on-chain transaction, and the service releases the other asset once your Litecoin confirms — no account and no custody. Because Litecoin runs its own proof-of-work network, an LTC swap is a cross-chain exchange rather than a token trade on a single chain: your Litecoin leaves the Litecoin network, and the asset you asked for arrives on its own.

Pick the pair and amount

Choose what you send (LTC) and what you receive. A live quote shows the rate and the estimated amount out before you commit.

Give a receiving address

Paste the wallet address for the asset you want back — on its correct network. This is where the swapped coin lands.

Send the LTC

Transfer Litecoin to the address shown, or confirm it in your connected wallet. One on-chain move, paid in low LTC network fees.

Receive after confirmations

Once the Litecoin transaction confirms, the service releases the target asset to your address. Timing depends on network congestion and the confirmations required.

Litecoin launched in 2011 as a Scrypt fork of Bitcoin and settles a block roughly every 2.5 minutes, so the LTC leg confirms fast and cheaply. Which two networks the swap connects decides the route, the fee, and the wallet you receive into.

Where to swap Litecoin

There are three main ways to swap Litecoin, and they trade off custody, speed, and how much of the process you control.

Instant swap services

Cross-chain exchangers quote a rate, take your LTC, and send the other asset to your address. No account, no custody held after the trade completes.

Centralized exchanges

Deposit LTC, trade on an order book, and withdraw. Deepest liquidity for large trades, but the exchange custodies your funds and usually requires sign-up.

In-wallet swap

Wallets such as Exodus or Trust Wallet have a built-in swap: pick LTC and the coin you want, and the app routes the exchange without moving funds elsewhere. Convenient for a quick swap from a balance you already hold.

Swap Litecoin to Bitcoin, USDT, Ethereum and other coins

Most Litecoin swaps head to one of a few destinations, and the mechanics change with the network the other asset lives on.

Swap LTC to BTC

Litecoin to Bitcoin is a like-for-like exchange between two proof-of-work chains — deep liquidity and a straightforward on-chain swap in either direction.

Swap LTC to USDT or USDC

Moving into a stablecoin converts your LTC into a dollar-denominated coin. USDT and USDC each run on several networks, so pick the network your wallet supports — the coins must arrive on the same chain you send them to.

Swap LTC to ETH, SOL or XRP

Swapping into another major coin sends your Litecoin out and delivers ETH, SOL or XRP to an address on that coin's own network. Keep a little of that chain's coin for its own fees.

Whatever the pair, the rule holds: the receiving address must belong to the destination asset's network, and the amount you get is set by the live LTC swap rate when the swap executes.

Litecoin swap fees and network cost

A Litecoin swap has two possible cost layers, and it helps to keep them separate. The Litecoin network fee is what miners charge to include your LTC transaction — it is famously low, typically a fraction of a cent to a few cents, and paid in LTC. On top of that, a service or spread may be built into the quoted rate for the exchange itself.

Because the LTC leg is cheap and fast, most of what you feel on a small swap is the rate itself. Always compare the quoted amount received across options rather than judging by a headline fee alone.

Litecoin swap problems and fixes

The swap hasn't arrived. Litecoin confirmations take time under load, and most services wait for several before releasing the other asset. Check your LTC transaction in a Litecoin block explorer; if it is confirmed, the delay is on the release side and usually resolves shortly.

Wrong network on the receiving side. The most common loss is sending to an address on the wrong chain. An LTC swap into, say, an ERC-20 asset must use an address on that asset's network. Confirm the network label before you submit.

Rate moved before you sent. Quotes are time-bound. If LTC price shifts between the quote and your send, the amount received can differ. Re-quote if you waited, and mind any minimum or maximum the service enforces.

Amount below the minimum. Very small swaps can be uneconomical once the fixed costs are covered. If a send is rejected or dust-sized on arrival, check the service's minimum before retrying.

Litecoin swap FAQ

What is the best way to swap Litecoin?

For most people a non-custodial instant swap is the quickest way: you get a live rate, send your LTC, and receive the other coin to your own address with no account. A centralized exchange suits large trades where you want an order book and deep liquidity.

Do I need an account to swap LTC?

Non-custodial swap services let you get a quote and exchange without signing up. Centralized exchanges generally require an account and custody your funds while you trade.

How long does a Litecoin swap take?

Litecoin blocks confirm about every 2.5 minutes, and services usually wait for a few confirmations before releasing the other asset. Total time depends on network congestion and the confirmations required.

What fee do I pay to swap Litecoin?

You pay the LTC on-chain network fee, which is typically very low, plus any spread the swap service builds into its rate. Compare the amount actually received rather than a headline fee.

What's the minimum amount of LTC I can swap?

Each service sets its own minimum, because very small swaps become uneconomical once fixed costs are covered. If a send is rejected or arrives dust-sized, check the service's minimum and adjust the amount before retrying.

Can I swap LTC directly for a token on another chain?

Yes — a cross-chain swap sends your LTC on the Litecoin network and delivers the target token to an address on its own network. Make sure the receiving address matches that token's chain.

Can I swap Litecoin to Bitcoin?

Yes. LTC to BTC is a like-for-like swap between two proof-of-work chains with deep liquidity, and it works the same way in reverse. You send Litecoin and receive Bitcoin at the quoted rate to your BTC address.

When is my Litecoin swap rate fixed?

It depends on the quote type. A fixed quote locks the rate for a short window, so you receive the shown amount if your LTC arrives in time; a floating quote settles at the market rate when your deposit confirms. Check which one the service shows before you send.

Before you swap Litecoin

A short checklist keeps a swap clean:

  • Match the networks. The receiving address must be on the target asset's own chain, not Litecoin's.
  • Keep a little LTC for fees if you plan to swap again from the same wallet.
  • Compare the amount received, not just the advertised fee, across a couple of routes.
  • Verify the service and address every time, and test a small amount on any route you haven't used before.
  • Pick the right stablecoin network — sending USDT or USDC to the wrong chain (ERC-20 vs TRC-20 and others) is a common, costly mistake.
